How to play

Rules: Guess the Logo with a Twist

  1. Everyone will be randomly divided into 3 groups using Spin the Wheel.
  2. Each group will take turns guessing the logo.
  3. If your group guesses correctly, you win a prize!
  4. If your group answers incorrectly, you must perform a random dance provided by the facilitators.
  5. If no group guesses correctly, we move on to the next logo.
  6. Everyone in the group must participate!
